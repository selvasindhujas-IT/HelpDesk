BLUETOOTH TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

CATEGORY: Bluetooth

OVERVIEW
Bluetooth issues generally fall into pairing failures, connection drops, and
devices not being discoverable.

PROBLEM: BLUETOOTH DEVICE WON'T PAIR
Possible Causes:
1. Bluetooth is turned off on one or both devices.
2. Device is already paired with another device.
3. Device is not in pairing/discoverable mode.
4. Outdated Bluetooth driver.

Basic Troubleshooting:
1. Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on both the computer and the target device.
2. Put the target device into pairing mode (usually a button press-and-hold).
3. Remove the device from the paired devices list and pair again from scratch.
4. Move the devices closer together (within 1-2 meters).

Advanced Troubleshooting:
1. Update the Bluetooth driver via Device Manager.
2. Restart the Bluetooth Support Service via services.msc.
3. Run the Windows Bluetooth Troubleshooter.
4. Check for Windows updates that may include Bluetooth stack fixes.

PROBLEM: BLUETOOTH KEEPS DISCONNECTING
Possible Causes:
1. Low battery on the Bluetooth device.
2. Interference from Wi-Fi or other 2.4GHz devices.
3. Power management settings turning off the Bluetooth adapter.

Solutions:
1. Charge or replace the batteries in the Bluetooth device.
2. Disable "Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power" for
   the Bluetooth adapter in Device Manager.
3. Move away from other wireless devices causing interference.
4. Update the Bluetooth driver to the latest version.

PROBLEM: BLUETOOTH NOT DISCOVERABLE / MISSING FROM SETTINGS
Possible Causes:
1. Bluetooth adapter disabled in Device Manager.
2. Missing or corrupted Bluetooth driver.
3. Airplane mode enabled.

Solutions:
1. Turn off Airplane Mode.
2. Enable the Bluetooth adapter in Device Manager if disabled.
3. Reinstall the Bluetooth driver from the manufacturer's website.
4. Restart the computer after driver installation.

RECOMMENDED FINAL SOLUTION
Removing and re-pairing the device, along with updating the Bluetooth driver,
resolves most Bluetooth connectivity problems.
